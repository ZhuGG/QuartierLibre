# Décor Guillotière

Asset final : `public/assets/guillotiere.png`. Génération avec l’outil imagegen intégré, sans API payante configurée dans le projet. Le décor a remplacé la première direction atelier au profit d’une ambiance de rue, sans monument emblématique ni personnage mascotte. L’illustration est utilisée en arrière-plan et sur les façades des bornes. Elle constitue une interprétation de l’ambiance du quartier, pas la reproduction d’une rue précise.

## Prompt final

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original production background mural for the side art of an adult-oriented indie arcade cabinet inspired by the everyday atmosphere of La Guillotiere, Lyon 7. Wide landscape around 1536x768, sophisticated authentic 16-bit pixel art, a continuous street elevation viewed at human level. Left half at late afternoon: closely packed ordinary five-story pale ochre and muted clay apartment facades, small independent street-level grocery and cafe storefronts, battered painted metal roller shutters, an open shop showing warm fluorescent interior lights, utilitarian awning, two mismatched small terrace chairs and round tables, bicycles locked to thin steel street hoops, modest utility boxes with tiny discreet paper stickers. Right half at dusk/night: the same street continues with a small neighborhood bar storefront, warm yellow light on pavement, a dark steel-blue shutter, a few imperfect window blinds, hanging cables and a couple of layered concert posters reduced to abstract print blocks with NO legible lettering.
